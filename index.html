    About

    Prabhakar Singh is Professor of Physics on the HAG scale at the Indian Institute of Technology (Banaras Hindu University), Varanasi, and served as Head of the Department of Physics from 2017 to 2020.

    He completed his M.Sc. in Physics and M.Tech. in Materials Science and Technology at Banaras Hindu University, and earned his Ph.D. magna cum laude from the University of Münster, Germany, working on broad-band conductivity spectroscopy of supercooled molten salts. Before joining IIT (BHU) in 2007, he taught for more than eight years at Udai Pratap College, Varanasi, and at IGNOU, New Delhi.

    Over more than two and a half decades he has published over 185 peer-reviewed papers, written a monograph and sixteen book chapters, co-edited Nanomaterials: Advances and Applications, and guided twenty doctoral students. He has completed ten externally funded projects as principal investigator and has been an invited speaker at conferences in Europe, North America and Asia.

    His institutional service includes Chief Proctor, Chairman of the Council of Wardens, Senate representative on the Board of Governors, member of the Finance Committee, and President of the Faculty Forum. He serves on boards of studies and senates of several universities and institutes across the country.
