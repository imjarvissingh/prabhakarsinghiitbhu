    About

    Prabhakar Singh is Professor of Physics on the HAG scale at the Indian Institute of Technology (Banaras Hindu University), Varanasi. He served as Head of the Department of Physics from 2017 to 2020.

    He completed his M.Sc. in Physics and M.Tech. in Materials Science and Technology at Banaras Hindu University and earned his Ph.D. magna cum laude at the University of Münster, Germany, working on broad-band conductivity spectroscopy of supercooled molten salts. He joined IIT (BHU) in 2007 after more than eight years of teaching at Udai Pratap College, Varanasi, and at IGNOU, New Delhi.

    In more than two and a half decades he has published over 185 peer-reviewed papers, a monograph and sixteen book chapters, co-edited Nanomaterials: Advances and Applications, and guided twenty doctoral students. He serves on the editorial boards of Scientific Reports and Frontiers in Electronic Materials and on the senates and boards of studies of several institutes and universities.
